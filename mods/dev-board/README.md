# dev-board

A Claude Code mod for a boss session in the Claude desktop app: a live board of the sessions it started (its workers), from `dev desktop board --json`.

- `/board` opens the **Workers** pane (the desktop app docks it in the right side panel), titled with its counts while open (`Workers · 3 · 1 needs you`), its workers in sections by status, each with its count beside it, empty ones hidden: **Needs you** (red), **Working** (working, stuck, rate-limited; accent, green on the desktop as its dot), **Waiting** (waiting, blocked, review ready; amber), **Idle** (idle, stopped; muted). Workers are rows without borders, a thin rule between them (a faint hairline on the desktop, a blank row on the terminal). Section color is only on a row's dot, a section's header and what a Needs you row says (red; in the detail the current task step keeps its section's color); notes and needs are amber, the PR and CI icons and the progress bar their own. Colors are the app theme's, so light and dark both work. On the desktop the to do list and sections sit `md` apart (`sm` off it); rows and a row's lines `xs`.
- Above the sections, the boss's **To do** list (`dev todo`, kept by the boss): a header (`TO DO 3 · 1 on others`, then `updated 12m` at the right, off the desktop dimmer once the list is unchanged an hour), then its first 5 items (`+N more` past them), each a row: its box (☐; ◌ and its text subtle for one waiting on someone else; ☑, its text struck through, for a ticked one while the list still shows it), its text cut to one row, then at the right who it waits on (`on Marek (Ops)`, amber), else its link: a PR its icon and `#87` (click to open), another link `↗`. Read-only: the boss ticks and adds. An empty list, or one that can't be read, shows no block.
- The first `/board` in a desktop session also asks it to act as boss: `dev-board: /board opened — load the dev-board:boss skill and act as boss for this session's workers.`, which starts a turn once it is idle (a prompt, not a command's hidden context, so the skill loads now rather than at the next message). It asks once per session: the mod keeps that in the session's state, so opening the pane again or a reload of the mod asks nothing; a prompt the app refuses shows as a toast and isn't retried.
- The mod bundles the boss rules as its `dev-board:boss` skill (`skills/boss/SKILL.md`): a copy of `internal/skill/desktop-boss/SKILL.md` named `boss`, which `go test ./internal/skill` checks and `go test ./internal/skill -update` rewrites. Edit the source, not the copy.
- A worker's row: its dot (●) beside its lines, colored by its section, not the app's state: red under Needs you, amber under Waiting, green under Working, grey under Idle, dimmer once stopped off the desktop (so a worker whose open dialog waits on the person is red, though the app has it busy). Its lines, a small gap apart:
  - line 1, never wrapping: the worker's name (its title when it has none), semibold, a plain button that opens or closes the detail; then what it says, in a smaller face, cut to fit, its markdown backticks and bold stripped, red and semibold under Needs you, else subtle; then, for Needs you and Waiting rows, **Open**, **Message…** and **Archive**; and at the right its time in its state (busy or idle; since its last activity once stopped: `12m`, `now` under a minute). What it says is one text, the first it has of: a question once its answer is sent (while asked, the question's own rows under line 1 say it); the status and what it says (`blocked · pick one`, `rate-limited · resumes 18:05`, `stuck · no progress for 17m`), the status left out where it repeats the section (`allow Bash: …` or `waiting for you` under Needs you, what its tool call says it does under Working (`Wait 20s, then count hook files`, a Bash or Agent call's `description`; else the tool, `Bash`; between its tools the first line of what it last said this turn, else its last finished call's `description`, else `working`, so the line stays; then its background commands and agents still running, `· 1 background running`), `Auto-fix: PR #80` under Waiting); the detail of the app's verdict on its last turn; the newest of its last lines (below). A blocked verdict the worker's own turn end contradicts (a wrap_up with no ask, not blocked, its recap asking nothing, no reply of the turn ending on a question, then the bare `IDLE` marker: the person answered, and the app's summary mislabelled that turn) is dropped by `dev`, its detail and need with it: the worker is idle (stopped when not running) and, unless something else keeps its row, joins the idle line; with the `dev` v0.50.0 build it still shows until the next release;
  - its question, if it has one, under line 1 (below);
  - meta line, in a smaller face, muted, the parts it has split by `·` and wrapping when narrow (none, no line): the model (`opus`, `sonnet`, `haiku`, `fable`, else the app's id) and its effort (`opus·medium`), the context use (`9%`), each PR, then the task progress: a small bar (done steps green on a faint track, on the terminal a thin dim one, so an empty bar reads as empty) and `done/total` (`2/5`). A PR is its state icon (open green, merged purple (grey on the desktop: done), closed red, each its own shape), `PR #93` (click to open), then for an open PR its CI icon from `gh pr checks` (✓ passed or skipped, ✗ any failed or cancelled, else a clock while any runs; none until read or with no checks) and the names of the checks that make it (the failed ones, the running ones, else all; cut to fit). The icons are SVG drawn for this mod, their `alt` read aloud (`PR #93 open`, `CI failed`); the terminal draws glyphs (○ ◆ ✕, ✓ ✗ …) in the theme's colors;
  - health notes and what it needs (`needs: …`; not beside a question's options, which ask it), a line each with ⚠, always shown;
  - the Message… field, while open;
  - the detail, opened by the name: the session title where it differs from the name (subtle); the branch in a mono face, cut at its start so its unique end shows; the task list from the worker's update_plan, a row per step, its mark and its text cut to one row: ● done (green, dimmed; on the desktop mark and text muted, not dimmed), ◐ current (in the section's color, its text stronger), ○ pending (with more than 7 steps, the leading done steps but the last fold into `● n done`, and the steps past the room into `○ n more`; the current step always shows); the last two lines of its newest reply (dim, muted on the desktop; code fences, turn-end markers such as `IDLE`, a line repeating the status (its background count aside), its question or the need its options answer, and one naming every option among list words only (`Pick a reply: Per package, or Total only.`; not `Per package is slower; Total only skips files.`) skipped) without list markers, backticks or bold, a link as its text, each cut to one row; where no reply line shows (e.g. its reply was only a marker), the last turn's wrap_up recap, dim (muted on the desktop) and wrapped (from a `dev` newer than v0.50.0); `not delivered: <why>`; and, for Working and Idle rows, **Open**, **Message…** and **Archive**. Whether a row's detail is open sticks across polls and status changes for the session; closed by default.

  Idle and stopped workers share one line, `▸ name, name, …` (cut to fit; the IDLE header already counts them), or `▸ 2 more · name, name` below idle rows of their own, that opens to a line each: dot, name, task count, time. Its name opens that worker's row and detail. On the desktop the open line counts them only (`▾ 4 idle`, `▾ 2 more`), as its lines name them, and those names are muted and regular: they ask nothing. An idle worker with a health note, a need or a question, one being messaged, or one whose detail is open keeps its own row; closing its detail puts it back in the line.
- Buttons per worker (**Open**, **Message…** and **Archive** text buttons, dim on the terminal and phones, full strength on the desktop so they don't read as the status beside them):
  - **Open** opens the worker's session in the app (`claude://code/continue?session=<id>`); on line 1 for Needs you and Waiting rows, in the detail for Working and Idle ones, as are **Message…** and **Archive**.
  - **Message…** opens a field (labelled `Message <name>`) with **Send** and **Cancel**; Enter or Send asks the boss to relay its text: `dev-board: message <name> (<id>): <text>`; a message the app refuses opens the field again, holding its text, and on the desktop says under it `not sent: press Send to try again, or Cancel` (the toast says why).
  - **Archive**, shown once `dev delivered <worktree>` exits 0 for a worker with a PR and a worktree that is neither busy nor waiting on something that will wake it (a `WAITING (…)` marker; such a worker shows no Archive), else the detail says `not delivered: <why>`; checks it again and asks the boss to archive it: `dev-board: archive <name> (<id>): dev delivered exited 0`. A worker going busy drops its last check.

  The prompts wait for the boss to go idle; a toast says each was queued, or why not. A worker's permission prompt can't be answered here: answer it in its session (**Open**).
- A worker's question (AskUserQuestion) shows under its row's line 1, in place of what line 1 says: each question's header and text, its options buttons in one row that wraps when narrow, or, where any option has a description, one per row with the description beside its button in a smaller face (cut to one row; the label not repeated); an option ending in `(Recommended)` shows without it and a `★` before (one question with one pick: that one leads, else the first; else the picked ones). The pick sent is the option's own label, suffix included. One question with one pick: pressing an option answers it. A multi-select (☐/☑) or several questions (○/◉ each): pick, then **Send**, which says which question still lacks a pick. The answer is the person's pick, as the dialog would send it (a multi-select's labels comma-joined), and the engine aborts the worker's own dialog; a pick there first wins instead, and the board's answer is refused. The row then says `sent: …; waiting for the worker` under line 1, and line 1 the question, until it is gone.

  How: every desktop session loads this mod, so a worker's own copy sees its AskUserQuestion. While the dialog is open it writes the question to `questions/<app session id>.json` in dev's state directory (`~/.local/state/dev/desktop/`); the board runs `dev desktop answer <id> <tool use id>`, which writes `<id>.answer.json` (atomically, only for the question still open), and the worker's copy answers with it. Both files go once it is answered, either way. `dev desktop board` removes those its mod left behind (a reload, a crash): of a worker no longer running (once the file is a minute old: one started since the board read the live files may have just written it), or running no turn, or whose transcript has the answer; and anyone's older than a week. A question read only from the transcript (a worker whose mod is older, or could not write the file) can't be answered here: its options open its session, and the row says so. A permission prompt still can't be answered here: **Open**.
- A worker whose last turn ended on a wrap_up question (`ask`) shows it the same way, its reply buttons the options (of the first two actions, the ones the app draws; app actions such as Commit left out), and it keeps its own row even when idle. A blocked one (`blocked: true`) puts the worker under Needs you, the question its status, whatever its last lines say (an `IDLE` marker says only that nothing runs). Pressing one answers it as the person would in the worker's session: the board writes the label to `questions/<id>.reply.json` and the worker's own copy of the mod submits it as its next prompt, the person's own words. That route is taken while the worker's copy listens for that ask (it rewrites `questions/<id>.listen` every 2 s, naming the wrap_up its last turn ended on; `dev desktop board` marks the ask `listening` while that file is under 10 s old, names it, and the worker runs); else the press asks the boss to message the worker that label (`dev-board: message …`, as **Message…** does). The worker takes a reply once, and only for the wrap_up its last turn ended on: one for an earlier ask, one written over a minute ago, or one arriving once a turn started is dropped. A reply the worker still waits for 10 s on (not taken, or its prompt refused) brings the buttons back, with a toast. Without reply buttons it is answered with **Message…**. It goes once the worker gets a message or starts a turn; a busy worker's open AskUserQuestion is newer and shows instead.
- Health notes (stuck, a decision unanswered, high context, rate-limited, resume due) show under their row. A note the boss hasn't had yet goes to it as one prompt per poll for all new ones, e.g. `dev-board: fix-login stuck: no progress for 17m; docs-api resume due: rate limit reset 18:05: resume it`, which starts a boss turn once it is idle. The keys sent are kept in the session's state, so a reload of the mod sends none again; a prompt the app refuses shows as a toast and isn't retried.
- The band above the prompt sums them up, e.g. `1 needs you · 3 workers · 1 busy · 1 blocked · PR #78 open` (a worker that needs you counted there only, not busy or blocked too), dim, its `needs you` part red; hidden when there are none (or while a survey shows).

Every 2 s it runs `dev desktop board --json --boss $CLAUDE_CODE_HOST_SESSION_ID` and `dev todo --json --boss` the same id (one that fails, as a `dev` without `dev todo` or a worker's session does, shows no block and says why once, in the debug log); `gh pr checks` per open PR and `dev delivered` per worker not busy with a PR run at most once a minute each. Without `gh` on the app's `PATH` the PR on the meta line says why. A failure (no `dev`, an old `dev` without the command, no app record for the session) shows its error in the pane, with a ⚠ and that it retries every 2 s; with no error and no workers it says `No workers yet`; files `dev` could not read show as warnings above the rows, a ⚠ footnote row each, as does a live session status it doesn't know (that worker shows busy). A desktop session loads the mod once, when its Claude starts (unless `CLAUDE_CODE_PLUGIN_DIR_WATCH=1`, which reloads it): once the files it loads (`.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, `hooks/`, `skills/`) are newer, the board warns (`dev-board mod changed 19:17, after this session started 17:27: start a new session to load it`): it runs the older copy; start a new session (or restart the app) to load them. Outside a desktop app session (no `CLAUDE_CODE_HOST_SESSION_ID`) it runs no `dev`; the pane says why.

## Desktop look

The desktop pane draws from a fixed set of tokens (named constants at the top of `hooks/register.tsx`), the terminal and the phones keeping their own looks:

- colors (`TONE`): text, muted (`subtle`), ok (`success`), caution (`warning`), danger (`error`): theme keys, so light and dark follow the theme. The SVG icons, which can't name a theme key, draw the same tones in hues about 4.2:1 on both the light and the dark pane (`TONE_HEX`; a merged PR muted). Nothing dimmed: a receding line, and the idle line's toggle, are muted. No gradients; the bar's track and the hairline between rows are a neutral grey at 22% (`BAR_TRACK`);
- type (`SIZE`, `WEIGHT`): body and footnote; regular and semibold (a caption or a medium weight elsewhere is footnote or semibold here);
- spacing (`SPACE`): 1 cell across a row, `xs` between rows and a row's lines, `sm` around a notice, `md` between the to do list, warnings and sections.

The layout reads no width: it shrinks the name and what line 1 says (cut to fit), wraps the meta line, and keeps the buttons and the time, which fit a 390 px pane. Focus rings, keyboard order and the buttons' size are the app's.

## Load it

1. A `dev` build with `dev desktop board` on the app's `PATH`. The desktop app's `PATH` may lack `~/go/bin`: if the pane says `dev` can't start, set `DEV_BIN` at the top of `hooks/register.tsx` to its absolute path.
2. Name this folder in `CLAUDE_CODE_PLUGIN_DIRS` in the `env` block of `~/.claude/settings.json` (absolute paths, `:`-separated; never a project's settings):

   ```json
   { "env": { "CLAUDE_CODE_PLUGIN_DIRS": "/path/to/dev/mods/dev-board" } }
   ```

3. Start a new desktop session (or restart the app) and run `/board` in the boss session.

## Develop

```bash
claude plugin validate mods/dev-board
claude plugin test mods/dev-board
```

The engine writes the API types into `.claude-plugin/types/` (ignored by git) once it loaded the mod; `tsc -p mods/dev-board` type-checks it then.
